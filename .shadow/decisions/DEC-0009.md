## Context

Phaser still composes the game through registered scenes, but several scene files have grown into broad coordinators. `OverworldScene` remains about 1,359 lines in this worktree, while nearby helpers such as `OverworldInteraction` hold part of the interaction behavior.

## Decision

Treat scenes as lifecycle and presentation coordinators. Durable state, reusable gameplay rules, entity behavior, and cross-scene services belong in managers, systems, entities, battle modules, or UI widgets rather than being reimplemented inside each scene.

## Rationale

The runtime registry in `game-config.ts` makes scenes the composition boundary, and repository guidance directs domain logic to battle, systems, or managers while scenes coordinate behavior and rendering. Existing overworld helpers show the intended decomposition path without removing Phaser scene ownership.

## Consequences

New scene work should introduce or reuse focused collaborators instead of growing scene-local subsystems. Existing large scenes are allowed to remain until touched, but changes should move stable rules outward when the behavior can be verified without Phaser lifecycle state.

## Unknowns

This boundary is only partially realised. `OverworldScene` is still large, and concurrent scene refactors may change helper names and exact responsibilities after this record is written.
