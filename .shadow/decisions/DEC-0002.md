## Context

The browser entry point constructs one Phaser game from `gameConfig`. That
configuration registers boot, title, overworld, battle, menu, Pokemon, and
minigame scenes.

## Decision

Use Phaser scenes as the runtime composition units. Scenes own lifecycle-bound
presentation and coordinate focused helpers for their domain.

## Rationale

The scene registry is the concrete application composition root. The
architecture also describes overlay and parallel scene relationships for menus,
dialogue, and battle UI.

## Consequences

New scene behavior must follow Phaser lifecycle boundaries and scene
registration. Shared or persistent concerns belong outside individual scenes.
