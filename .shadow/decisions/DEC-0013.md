## Context

Battle outcomes combine rules, random rolls, presentation queues, and Phaser animation. Deterministic tests need control over rule randomness without depending on scene rendering.

## Decision

Keep battle rule code under `frontend/src/battle/`, inject `BattleRng` through managers, calculators, effect handlers, AI, catch logic, and move execution, and leave scene code responsible for presentation and user-facing orchestration.

## Rationale

`BattleManager` accepts `rng` or `rngSeed`, exposes `getRng()`, and passes the RNG into status handling. Calculators and `MoveExecutor` accept `BattleRng`, while the battle RNG determinism test proves seeded sequences replay and diverge by seed.

## Consequences

New battle rules should accept or obtain the active `BattleRng` instead of calling `Math.random` or global helpers directly. Scene presentation can remain Phaser-driven, but rule determinism should be testable outside the scene lifecycle.

## Unknowns

This is partially realised. `BattleUIScene` still owns significant turn orchestration, and `MoveAnimationPlayer` still uses `Math.random` for visual particles; that visual randomness is not part of battle rule determinism.
