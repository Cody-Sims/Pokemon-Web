## Context

Turn execution has many ordered states, terminal outcomes, calculations, and
effects. Scene code separately presents sprites, menus, messages, and rewards.

## Decision

Isolate battle rules under `frontend/src/battle/` and drive battle flow through
an explicit finite state machine. Battle scenes compose presentation around that
subsystem.

## Rationale

`BattleManager` and `DoubleBattleManager` register and transition FSM states.
The FSM centralizes enter, update, exit, and terminal-state behavior, and focused
unit tests verify transition semantics.

## Consequences

Battle changes must preserve registered transitions and subsystem boundaries.
Behavioral checks can exercise battle logic without requiring a running Phaser
scene.
